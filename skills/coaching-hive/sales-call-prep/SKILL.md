# Sales call prep

## Steps
1. Gather: booking answers, DM thread, form, anything the Setter saved.
2. Write one page:
   - **Situation**: where they are now, in their words
   - **Goal**: what they want and by when
   - **Pain**: why now
   - **Fit**: which offer in `~/second-brain/offer.md` fits, and why
   - **Likely objections** with the coach's answers from `~/second-brain/objections.md`
   - **5 questions to ask**
3. Mark anything unconfirmed with `?`.
