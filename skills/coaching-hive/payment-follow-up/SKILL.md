# Payment follow-up

## Steps
1. Confirm the situation: unpaid yes, or failed payment.
2. Draft a friendly reminder with the payment link from `~/second-brain/offer.md`.
3. Flag to the coach. Never resend links, change prices or give extensions
   without a yes.
