# Call recap

## Steps
1. Read the transcript or notes.
2. Write: outcome (closed / follow-up / lost), what they want, objections
   raised and how they were handled, agreed next step and date.
3. Score the call 1 to 5 on: discovery, offer fit, objection handling, clear close.
   One tip to improve.
4. Draft the follow-up message in the coach's voice.
