# Objection coach

## Steps
1. Ask which objection to practise, or pick the most common in `~/second-brain/objections.md`.
2. Play the lead. Stay realistic, not hostile.
3. After 3 to 5 exchanges, stop and score: listened, asked, answered, closed.
4. Give one better line the coach could use, and offer to save it.
